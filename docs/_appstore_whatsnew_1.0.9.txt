<en-US>
Display fixes

- The Endless Buff Pass now shows its remaining days in the shop and buff panel
- Buff timers are shown in a short form (for example 1h22m)
- While the pass is active the timer shows the infinity mark
- Long buff timers no longer cover the power and currency readouts
- Rewards sent by the operator arrive in your mailbox
