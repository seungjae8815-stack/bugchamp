## English

### Title
```
Update 1.0.18 — Training & duel overhaul, bigger gifts
```

### Body
```
Update 1.0.18 is here. (iOS after review)

[ Rewards ]
- Gifts: 1-3 hours of hunting. Daily rewards: 3h at lunch, 5h at dinner, plus a claim-again bonus
- Exchange: 10 jelly = 5 hours of hunting
- Faster early Easy zones; Attack, HP and Defense upgrades go up to 250

[ Training ]
- Bugs earn training points to spend across 10 slots (recommended allocation button included)
- Old upgrades carry over as points, so no bug gets weaker. First reset is free
- Duel stones change a bug's element or temperament

[ Duels ]
- Tap counter: mash the screen before a ring-out, flip or knockout to hold on. Grit makes taps stronger
- Size now works only as weight, so big bugs no longer win one-sidedly
- Applies to King Bug Contest round 2 too (until Oct 18)

[ More ]
- Auto boss challenge (can be turned off in Settings)
- Movement speed is now Evasion; gear can roll Evasion
- Fairy box expansion with jelly (up to 60)
- New shop packs; better Starter Pack and skin perks
```
