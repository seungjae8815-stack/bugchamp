---------- English ----------
Duel tickets
Each duel now costs one ticket. You get one back every 30 minutes, up to 10.
You can also top up with an ad or with jelly.
Trophies now reflect how strong your bugs are, not how long you played.

Notices
Read game news right inside the app. A dot appears when something new arrives.

Mail and gift codes
Claim gifts such as maintenance rewards from your mailbox, and redeem gift codes in Settings.

If you bought Remove Ads
Rewarded ads are now skipped too - you get the reward straight away.

Sound is here
• 20+ new sound effects for collecting, exploring and events
• New background music for the arena and special stages
• Toggle BGM and SFX separately, with volume control in Settings
• Fixed an issue where no sound played

More improvements
• Roadmap redesign - clearer stage markers and text
• See your rank and how it changed when you sign in
• Collection limit - 50 bugs, expandable to 100 with jelly
  Over the limit? Your best bugs are kept, so pin your favorites first
• Messages now appear in the middle of the screen
• Fixed the last row of the ranking being cut off

Thanks for playing!
