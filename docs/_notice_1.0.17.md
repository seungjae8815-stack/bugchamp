### Body
```
Update 1.0.17 is here. (Android now; iOS after review)

[ Changes ]
- If a regular monster defeats you in the highest zone you can reach, you fall back one zone
  · You arrive with the boss challenge open. Beat that boss to go right back
  · Zone 1 never drops you to the previous difficulty (only the gauge resets)
  · In a lower zone picked from the roadmap, only the gauge resets
- In the Abyss you fall back one floor. The weekly Abyss ranking and progress ranking use your current floor
- Exchange: 10 jelly gives the gold or materials of one hour of hunting at your current power (much more than before)
- Buttons that spend jelly now ask for confirmation first (expansions, instant finish, rerolls, ticket refills, etc.)

[ Fixes ]
- Training being canceled when you left the game right after starting it
- Tap anywhere on the stacked gear in the workshop to open the newest item
- Various text and bug fixes

Bug King Trials Round 2 is on now! (until Oct 18)
```
