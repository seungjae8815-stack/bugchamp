- 새 지역 잿불 능선과 보스 잿불의 군주가 등장합니다

장비를 고르는 재미

- 장비의 고정 능력치를 없애고 무작위 옵션 두 줄로 바꿨습니다
- 옵션은 한 줄씩 따로 다시 굴릴 수 있어요. 이미 착용한 장비도 됩니다
- 제련에 등급 필터와 자동 망치질 횟수 선택이 생겼습니다
- 원하는 장비가 나오면 자동으로 멈추게 할 수 있어요
- 망치질 가속과 모루 칸 확장을 추가했습니다

고친 것

- 순위표를 여는 것만으로 내 진행도가 처음으로 되돌아가던 문제

- 능력치 강화에 상한을 표시합니다
- 부화기 알 목록에서 특성과 잠재력, 속성을 바로 볼 수 있어요

<en-US>
Your bugs fight with you

- Bugs on your team now strike monsters themselves, each at its own pace
- Every region has an element. Bring bugs with the winning element for much bigger damage
- Bugs take damage and go down, then get back up after a while
- Twenty monsters were redrawn, with names and attack and hurt motions
- Elite monsters appear now and then: tougher, but far better rewards
- New region Ember Ridge and its boss, the Ember Lord

Gear worth choosing

- Fixed gear stats are gone. Every piece now rolls two random options
- Each option can be rerolled on its own, including on gear you already wear
- Forging gained a grade filter and a strike-count picker
- Auto forging can stop as soon as the gear you want appears
- Added hammer speed-up and more anvil slots

Fixes

- Opening the leaderboard reset your own progress to the start
- Players outside the top 50 were shown as rank 51
- Very large numbers ran off the screen
- Critical and material find did nothing past a certain level
- Stat upgrades now show their cap
- The egg list shows traits, potential and element at a glance

Entomologist Pass changed

- 16h offline cap, +30% idle gold, 20 jelly daily
</en-US>

- 地域ごとに五行の属性がつきました。相性の良い昆虫を連れて行くと威力が大きく上がります

- ときどき現れる精鋭モンスターは硬い代わりに報酬が大きいです
- 新地域の燠火の尾根とボスの燠火の王が登場します

装備を選ぶ楽しさ

- 装備の固定能力をなくし、ランダムな2つのオプションに変えました

- ほしい装備が出たら自動で止められます
- ハンマーの加速と金床の枠拡張を追加しました
