Bug art redrawn to match the real insects

- The giant, flat and two-spotted stag beetles, lesser rhino beetle, relict and saw longhorns, jumping mantis, longheaded grasshopper and stag-horned chafer now look like the real species. The albino stag beetle and golden rhino beetle skins follow the new look
